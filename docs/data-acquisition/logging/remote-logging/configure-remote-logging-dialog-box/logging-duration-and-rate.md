# Logging duration and rate

The estimated duration of the remote logging session is displayed in the **Logging Duration** panel. The calculation is based on the sample rates of all the enabled parameters in all channels, and on the settings in the Logging Duration dialog box. Click **Setup** to open the [Logging Duration](../duration.md) dialog box.

The **Logging** and **Telemetry** boxes in the **Rate** panel show the data rates required for logging and for telemetry. They are calculated from the parameters enabled in the channels that are logged to the unit or sent by telemetry, and their individual sampling frequencies.

**See also:** [Remote Logging - Duration](../duration.md); [Remote Logging - Channel Overview tab](channel-overview-tab.md)
