# Rotating a Graphical view

The three-dimensional graphical views of 2 axis Maps can be rotated with the mouse or the Map Bar buttons:

- **Map Bar buttons** - **Rotate clockwise** and **Rotate anti-clockwise** turn the display in 10 degree steps; **Tilt forwards** and **Tilt backwards** tilt it. See [Map Bar](../../using-system-monitor/tool-and-information-bars/map-bar.md).
- **Mouse** - Click anywhere in the map window (take care not to select a value) and drag the cursor. The front of the display follows the cursor movement.
